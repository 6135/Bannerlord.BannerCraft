Changelog
v1.4.6
Built against each supported game version (1.3.4 - 1.4.8) instead of always against 1.3.13.
Fixed the smelt and refine buttons being enabled without a valid selection (smelting/refining did nothing) and smelting being blocked by the weapon crafting stamina cost.
Fixed locked and duplicated items in the smelting list when Better Smithing is installed.
"Allow smelting other items" is now respected when Better Smithing is installed.
Items with different modifiers are now listed separately in the smelting list, with their full name (e.g. Rusty, Lordly).
Fixed a crash when opening the inventory after a save/reload with items crafted from runtime weapon designs, and repaired items affected by it.
Crafting failures (botches) now show a clearer message, including in the message log.
Botching a weapon no longer uses stamina twice, respects the no materials/stamina/difficulty cheats, and can't happen without the required materials.
Fixed the Done button overlapping the item image in the armor crafting result popup, and the popup title not showing.
Enter now crafts and confirms in armor crafting mode.
Fixed the uncloseable popup in the smithy with Banner Kings / Banner Kings Redux installed.
Reduced the material cost of crossbows.
Malformed items from other mods no longer break the armor crafting list.

v1.0.26.0
Added 3 cheats. 

1) sets material costs to 0. 
2) sets stamina cost to 0.
3) sets item difficulty to 0 (removing botch chances and increasing chances at better results)

v1.0.25.0

Updated to 1.2.4 and made it easier to keep old version compatible code when updating by reversing the conditions on the preprocessor directives. Fixed a visual and functional but where the previous extra crafting materials where shown when switching to normal crafting. Possible fix for a nullRefExeption on updating materials (temporary fix).

v1.0.22
Refactored modifier behaviour using vanilla formulas, and added the option to switch between behaviours. Added options to increase an individual modifier's chance of showing up. Fixed a major bug when crafting vanilla items.
